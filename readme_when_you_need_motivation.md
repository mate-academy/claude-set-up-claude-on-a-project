# A Little Motivation

If you're reading this, you're probably in the middle of something hard — a bug that won't die, a feature that won't come together, or just a day that feels heavier than it should. That's okay. That's normal. It doesn't mean you're doing it wrong.

Progress rarely looks like progress while you're in it. It looks like confusion, dead ends, and one small fix at a time. But every one of those small fixes adds up, and the version of you a week from now will barely remember how stuck you felt right now.

You don't have to solve everything today. You just have to take the next honest step.

> "It always seems impossible until it's done." — Nelson Mandela

Take a breath. Drink some water. You've got this.
